Privacy Policy for Tauric Research

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Tauric Research Chrome Extension ("Extension") provided by Tauric Research ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our extension.

1. Information We Collect

To provide you with our AI trading analysis services, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Email address and securely hashed password used for authentication and Tauric Pro subscriptions.
  • Usage Data: The financial ticker symbols (e.g., BTC/USDT, AAPL) you actively request to analyze via the extension.

2. How We Use the Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate users and manage access to free tiers and Pro features.
  • To process your requested ticker symbols through our Multi-Agent AI engine and return the analysis report.
  • To track daily usage limits for free-tier users.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. Your data is used exclusively to operate the core functionalities of the Tauric Research extension.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information:

  • All communication between the extension and our backend API is encrypted using secure protocols (HTTPS/TLS).
  • User passwords are securely hashed before being stored in our database; we do not store plaintext passwords.
  • Authentication tokens are temporary and managed securely.

5. Chrome Web Store Policies Compliance

We comply with all Chrome Web Store Developer Program Policies. This extension strictly follows the "Single Purpose Policy" focusing on financial AI chart analysis. We only request the minimum permissions necessary (such as storage and activeTab) to function.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. By continuing to use the extension after those changes become effective, you agree to be bound by the revised policy.
